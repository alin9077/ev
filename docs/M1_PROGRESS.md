## Research completion and coverage qualification

The report distinguishes the research queue from the strict coverage targets. There is no editable “complete” flag.

A brand is **research-complete** when its dated complete current-version list is established, every known model/lead difference is resolved, every on-sale core field has an earned grade (including a completed two-source unsuccessful search), its Tier 1 strengths/trade-offs are supported, and its claims are fresh. Two-source “Not published” gaps allow research to move to the next brand. Missing 10–80% charging time when only 30–80% is published, for example, can be a finished research outcome while C2 and C5 remain open. Whole-market independent-list closure is not required before moving to the next brand.

A brand is separately **coverage-qualified** only when it meets all six criteria:

1. Every current version and inherited audit/lead disposition is reconciled; the two independent market-list snapshots are valid, and all differences relevant to that brand are settled
2. Price is filled for every on-sale version; each other core field is at least 95% filled
3. Every on-sale core cell has an auditable M1 grade; each field below half Confirmed has an explanation, without a grade quota
4. Every Tier 1 version has a supported strength and trade-off
5. Each required ranking includes at least 90% of that brand’s on-sale versions
6. No Tier 1 current claim is aged, invalid or future-dated

The JSON includes each brand’s criteria, current-version reviews, exact blockers, counts, `researchCompleted` and its research-completed-as-of date, plus separate all-six-criteria completion and its as-of date. A fully reconciled exclusion can close an audit lead without adding a vehicle. A brand containing only excluded leads is not presented as a completed on-sale brand. The compact report lists brands done/remaining in the research queue and identifies coverage-qualified brands separately. A new or stale fact, model or version can reopen the relevant criterion.

## C1 registry: dated evidence, never inferred closure

`data/m1-market-reconciliation-2026-10-14.json` has the typed structure declared in `scripts/reporting/m1-reconciliation.ts`:

- `schemaVersion` and `checkedAt`: registry format and check date
- `publishedLists`: complete published Australian new Tier 1 BEV model-list snapshots. Each records its publisher, publication/check dates, original URL, complete model/body entries, an independence key and the reason it is independent
- `currentVersionReviews`: complete official Australian current-version lists, with exact model/body identity, model ID, check date, official URLs and reason. Every version identifies its catalogue ID, exact model, trim and model year. Optional `publishedModelAliases` records source-bound naming equivalences with a reason and URLs; it never relaxes body/trim/year identity
- `listResolutions`: one dated, sourced included/excluded/unresolved disposition for every published-list entry
- `catalogueOnlyDifferences`: a dated, sourced explanation for each reviewed model absent from each independent list
- `inventoryResolutions`: one sourced disposition for every inherited Tier 1 audit record and unverified lead, using its stable record/family ID (or brand-model fallback)

The inventory-only `included_split` outcome can resolve one inherited combined record/lead through at least two distinct complete current-version reviews. It preserves the entire original row exactly in `originalRecord`, including extra fields, and separately records a dated, sourced `declaredCoverage` containing every expected car, model, body, trim, model year and review identity. Each component must exactly match its valid current review and contain that review’s full version set; the union must equal the independently declared coverage. Duplicate, omitted, reused, stale or mismatched components fail. Existing body facts must agree; missing body facts are not invented. This is a source-reviewed inventory disposition, not automatic interpretation of a combined name. Ordinary published-list inclusion/exclusion matching, availability eligibility and on-sale denominators are unchanged; `included_split` is unavailable to published-list resolutions.

Two URLs from the same publisher or underlying dataset do not count as two independent lists. Snapshot capture must be complete, not cherry-picked to the existing catalogue. Research must establish independence; the report can validate the recorded structure, but it cannot independently prove that a researcher transcribed every published entry.

An included published entry must match the reviewed brand, model, body and vehicle class. Each reviewed version must match its actual catalogue ID, model ID, brand, model, trim and year. An explicit body-style fact, when present, must also match. Missing body evidence is not invented: the researcher must establish the body scope in the cited official current-version review. A sibling body, a different trim, a shared family prefix or a similar name cannot supply another version’s coverage.

Exclusions need a real date, evidence URLs, a reason and one of the supported dispositions: not sold new in Australia; not officially imported; not a BEV; outside Tier 1; not orderable or advertised as new stock; or duplicate of an explicitly matched model/body review. A duplicate must name the valid review. An on-sale catalogue model cannot also be excluded as not sold here. Unknowns remain unresolved.

Registry, version-list and resolution check dates use the 45-day availability window. Publication dates stay distinct. Future or invalid dates, duplicate IDs/resolutions, orphan references, an empty list, an unreviewed actual version, or an unresolved inherited audit item keep C1 open. Valid exclusions and complete current-version reviews must be recorded before the report will remove an inherited lead or unmatched model from its open-work list.

For later research, append the real snapshots/reviews and their source-bound resolutions to a new dated overlay, then update the report loader. Never invent entries to make counts agree, remove unresolved differences, or change the immutable snapshot to close a report.

## C3 earned grades

Coverage uses the shared `hasEarnedBuyerGrade` validator. A historic `standard` or `conditional` status alone no longer earns an M1 grade in the coverage report. A reviewed grade needs its dated overlay, reason, evidence scope and resolvable source citations. The report preserves filled values even when their grading review is unfinished, and shows them as Unreviewed in its split.

“Not published” requires an explicit unsuccessful search with two cited source URLs. A null value or old caveat is not such a search. It counts as a reviewed grade for C3, but it does not count as a filled field for C2. A value on the wrong charging window does not fill DC 10–80% time or earn a review for that requested core field.

The report gives the full Confirmed / Check / Reported / Not published / Unreviewed split for all 17 core fields and explains the recorded evidence patterns below half Confirmed. C3 can pass with a low Confirmed share when every grade is earned and the pattern is explained. It cannot pass by relabelling old statuses or writing a completion flag.
