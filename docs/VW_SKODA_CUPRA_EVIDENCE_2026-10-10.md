## Main release gates

- ID.4 Pro MY27 is 84/79kWh and GTX 82/77kWh. ID.5 MY26 is Pro82/77 and GTX84/79. These close siblings cannot share battery rows. Exact-MY brochures control over unversioned webpage range numbers. ID.5 document applicability says MY2026 despite a stale MY25.5 publication code.
- The MY27 Škoda PDFs are based on July 2026 production. Fresh MY27 Select85/Sportline/RS dealer cars built in June 2026 do not establish that July-sheet specifications apply to those VINs. The grade/MY advertisement and per-VIN specification match are distinct.
- Enyaq prices conflict: MY27 PDF $54,990/$63,990/$68,990 for Select60/Sportline85/Coupé Sportline85 versus live pages $55,990/$64,990/$70,990. Both have 31 December 2026 terms. Normalized values are null. RS $79,990 and Coupé RS $80,990 agree.
- MY27 Škoda60 uses61/58kWh LFP and105kW DC; MY26 Enyaq60 is63/59kWh NMC and165kW DC. MY27 EnyaqRS is82/77kWh,165kW/29min; MY26 RS is84/79kWh,185kW/26min. These updates must remain separate.
- Elroq Sportline85 is announced with October 2026 order timing, but its page still says final specifications pending. No completed ordering verification, MY27 specification column or clean exact stock was recovered.
- Buzz and Cargo pages now offer MY25 and MY26, but both download links remain explicitly MY25 brochures. MY26 offers/ads prove identities and prices, not wholesale MY25 technical or equipment inheritance. MY27 Pro+ and Cargo leads remain unresolved.
- Buzz MY25 consumption is printed207/211/212kWh/100km; Cargo prints203. Normalized consumption is null rather than silently correcting the unit. Cargo torque conflicts560/550Nm; GTX torque560–590Nm lacks a single rating. BuzzSWB folded cargo conflicts2205/2232L.
- Tavascan main page and March 2026 MY25.5 brochure use531km Endurance/505km VZ/477km Extreme; the older versions page uses534/499/463. Gross/usable capacities remain null because the AU booklet labels77kWh without a basis. Interior and Extreme are option packages, not independent grades.
- Refreshed MY26 TavascanV/VZ advertisements were retrieved, but their New category conflicts with demonstrator-sale wording. No clean new stock or exact currentMY26 manufacturer specification sheet was recovered. EnduranceMY26 remains a primary-source gap.
- Born’s manufacturer page explicitly says unavailable to order and sold out. No qualifying residual NEW stock or current Australian Born VZ was verified.
- Perth City Škoda has a fresh New MY27 Enyaq Select 60 ad: SK635964 / VIN TMBKK7NYXVF041492, 0 km, $56,490 drive-away. Its public stock feed claims build 8 July 2026 and September compliance. This clears the broad July production-date gate, but does not independently establish every per-VIN specification.
- CUPRA locator model codes are EXAE for Tavascan, EXBQ for Born and EXAI for Leon. The initial inventory API request used EXAI and returned 403; no Tavascan stock count was recovered. Generic app labels and old offer strings were not promoted to current inventory or pricing.
