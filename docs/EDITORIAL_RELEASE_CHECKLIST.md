# Owner checklist before public release

- Re-quote all published prices and critical specifications; preserve the original snapshot and add dated, sourced corrections.
- Resolve or retain each of the 15 publication issues, including Tesla V2L, L safety/build/software scope, EV3 pending notices, provisional AWD, Zeekr Black and EVO applicability.
- Independently verify any sales ranking before claiming national top ten.
- Confirm software period and eligibility with current evidence before extending the fixed observation window.
- Clear the working brand/name/domain; configure a verified correction contact and disclose future commercial relationships.
- Secure and record rights for any manufacturer photography. Permission must cover placements, edits, territory and expiry. Approval flags alone do not satisfy the gate.
- Check real phones, keyboards, zoom, screen readers and production browser print. Automated accessibility checks are not certification.
- Configure the real origin and verify canonical, sitemap, robots, CSP and hosting headers. Deploy only with explicit owner authorisation.
- Collect real-user LCP/INP/CLS only after an approved, lawful measurement setup. Lab numbers do not establish field performance.
