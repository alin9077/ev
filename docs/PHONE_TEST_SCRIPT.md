# Phone test script (about ten minutes)

For the owner, on an iPhone and then an Android phone. Use the preview link you were sent. It is a **noindex preview**, separate from the live site. Nothing you do is recorded; there are no accounts and no analytics.

Write down anything that felt slow, confusing or wrong, and the phone model. A stopwatch helps for step 3.

| # | Do this | You should see |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Open the preview link on your phone. | The Tesla Model Y heading first. A slim bar across the top: Find by need, Explore, Compare, Shortlist, Model Y pick. The page is short: scroll to the bottom, it should take only a few flicks. |
| 2 | Scroll to the list of cars. Type **Kia EV5** in the search box. | Cards for the EV5 versions. Each card shows a price with a "checked" date, a range in km, a third figure (charge time or seats), one strength, one trade-off, and whether it is on sale. |
| 3 | On an EV5 card, tap **Compare +**. Search **Sealion 7** and tap **Compare +** on a BYD Sealion 7. Tap **Compare selected**. | **Time yourself from step 2.** Under one minute, without scrolling past the Model Y heading to find anything. |
| 4 | On the comparison, find a figure with a small ◇ **Check** mark. Tap it. | A short condition opens in place (for example "needs the larger wheels"). Tap again to close it. |
| 5 | Find a figure in grey that says "Reported by …". | The number is still there, muted, with where it came from. |
| 6 | Find a price whose date is in amber. | The price is still shown. Only the date is amber, meaning it is due for a re-check. |
| 7 | Copy the page address from your browser's address bar and send it to yourself. | |
| 8 | Open that link on a desktop computer. | The same two cars, side by side. |
| 9 | Back on the phone: tap **Model Y pick** in the top bar, then tap the first of the nine reasons. | You land on that reason's detail. |
| 10 | Tap **Shortlist** and **Find by need**. | Shortlist opens the comparison saved on this phone, or the car list if nothing is saved. Find by need lists cars by the one thing you want most (range, price, space…). |

## Tell us

- Did step 3 take under a minute? How long?
- Anything you could not find, or that read like jargon?
- Anything that looked cut off, overlapped or jumped around?
- Would you trust the figures enough to start a shortlist? Why or why not?

The page footer says how many brands are fully reviewed and how many partly. A car from a brand that is only partly reviewed may show more grey "Reported" figures or "Unreviewed" labels. That is expected for the beta.
