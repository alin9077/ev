Checked 10 October 2026 (UTC). Research-only deliverable for Roadmark. All prices AUD.

## Results that change catalogue handling

- **12 current configurations across six families**, including three HiLux BEV body/grade combinations. All seat five.
- **Solterra is MY26; current Trailseeker and Uncharted are MY27.** Fresh public HTML, current variant identifiers and the 2027 brochure covers establish this. Search-index Subaru text still showed MY26. Four MY26 Trailseeker/Uncharted configurations remain separately documented for older-model-year NEW advertisements.
- **Uncharted has one marketing grade, AWD.** The panoramic-roof car is a factory-option configuration. Current claimed range, power, weights and ordinary cargo are unchanged; folded-to-ceiling cargo changes from 1,323 to 1,304 L and base configuration MLP from $59,990 to $61,190.
- Toyota uses calendar/model marketing descriptions and 2026 specification revisions, but the audited primary sources do not explicitly designate a formal MY26. The formal model-year field is therefore null.
- Current exact NEW dealer advertisements were verified for bZ4X AWD and both Solterra grades. The verified Trailseeker/Uncharted ads are MY26. Five other Toyota candidates remain cache-only. No advertisement establishes physical stock or factory-order acceptance.

## Warranty by applicable model/source

### Toyota current Australian terms (April 2026)

- Vehicle: 5 years/unlimited km for the ordinary private-use basis. Specified commercial uses are capped at 5 years/160,000 km.
- Electric motor/driveline: 5 years, potentially extended to 7 years with prescribed maintenance; commercial-use extensions excluded.
- Traction-battery defects: standard 5 years, renewable annually up to 10 years/unlimited km under the required battery-health inspections and operation/charging/maintenance conditions. Specified commercial uses do not receive that extension.
- Battery capacity is a separate guarantee: 8 years/160,000 km, whichever first, if energy capacity falls below 70%, subject to prescribed checks/use/maintenance. Specified commercial uses are excluded.
- Do not describe “10-year battery warranty” as an unconditional 10-year capacity-retention guarantee. First-delivery and use conditions matter, particularly for the HiLux fleet use case.

Source: [Toyota Warranty Advantage terms](https://www.toyota.com.au/-/media/project/toyota/toyota/owners/warranty/20260401_toyota-warranty-advantage_tandcs_t2026-022197.pdf?rev=a9c6245447e84c2fa4c0cff88b0c50c5), printed p3 warranty table and p4 notes 5a/5b (PDF zero-based pages 2/3). These terms do not remove Australian Consumer Law guarantees.

### Subaru MY26 Solterra / MY27 Trailseeker and Uncharted

- Vehicle: 5 years/unlimited km; listed commercial uses have a 5-year/150,000 km limit.
- High-voltage battery: 8 years/160,000 km. An explicit capacity-retention percentage was not established in the audited model-year source, so that threshold is null.
- 5 years/75,000 km is a **capped-price service program**, not free servicing. Roadside cover is 12 months and opt-in.
- The current Uncharted service footnote names Trailseeker MY27, apparently a template inconsistency. This is retained as a source qualification rather than rewritten as a clean model-specific guarantee.
- Older MY26 Trailseeker/Uncharted warranty entries come from their own retained MY26 specifications.
