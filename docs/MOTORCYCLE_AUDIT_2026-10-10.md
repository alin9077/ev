# Australian new electric motorcycle and motor scooter census

Reconstructed and reverified on 2026-10-10. 39 model-audit records across 18 brands, 63 unresolved lead groups and 12 exclusion groups. The existing 185-record inventory was checked read-only and contains no motorcycles or duplicate model families. This is not a closed market universe.

## Availability interpretation

24 records have advertised new-order offers, 1 has an explicit preorder, and 14 have unconfirmed current new supply. 6 have retrieved new-stock advertisements. These counts do not mean every model or configuration is physically available.

Honda WN7 remains a future subscription EOI. Registration is a supplier claim, not an independently checked VIN/state approval. All numerical rankings remain disabled. Retrieval times are retained and do not imply that a page or stock count was updated at that moment.

## Scope

Road motorcycles, seated motor scooters/mopeds and exact road-approved enduro/supermoto variants are included. E-bikes, kick scooters, personal mobility, off-road-only and youth competition bikes, and concepts are excluded. Approval evidence for one variant cannot be transferred to a similarly named off-road sibling.

## Key qualifications

- Stark EX/SM stock evidence is a named dealer configuration feed, without VINs. SKÖLL is sold out; EX Factory-specific availability remains unresolved.
- Sur-Ron Ultra Bee HP R and T have AU road claims; only T is specifically present in the retrieved order shop. Light Bee X and Light Bee 2 are off-road-only.
- Talaria Komodo L3E is explicitly separated from Off-Road but dealer page says sold out. Generic legal/specification templates are not reliable variant evidence.
- Kawasaki dealer MY26 listings coexist with MY24 maker pages. Deposits and catalogue identifiers do not prove physical new stock.
- Zero and Energica legacy/future supply conflicts stay visible. Honda WN7 is not an outright-sale offer.
- UBCO 2X2 Adventure and Special Edition share one road-family record; NEW unit condition remains unverified. SE MY2023 and road-equipment requirements stay configuration-specific.
- LEKI MY26.5 is a text designation; a fractional calendar year is not invented. Unknown years remain null.
- AE4 has no successfully retrieved dealer stock proof. AE6+/AE8+ use AU CFMOTO configuration data, avoiding unrelated dealer specification templates.

## Open work and comparison rules

The JSON preserves unresolved current-new supply, legacy variants and global-only leads. There is no exhaustive national importer/dealer or approval-to-sale reconciliation. No VIN approval audit was performed. Compare only compatible vehicle subtypes and measurement standards: WMTC, WLTP, steady-speed and ride-hour range are distinct. Keep power/torque definitions, price/on-road/battery inclusion and cargo measurement basis separate. Compatible ties must share rank under a declared method.

## Evidence files

`motorcycle-ev-census.json` contains every source URL, fresh-retrieval qualification, unresolved lead, excluded group, source-file hash and validation result. Four companion source-group JSON/Markdown pairs preserve the detailed evidence. Production application code and data were not edited. Append these motorcycle-only records to the latest base after collision checks; do not replace the base or overwrite newer MG4 evidence.
