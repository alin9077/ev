# Roadmark continuing goal

Make Roadmark the clearest way to discover what every new EV available in Australia does best, and compare the vehicles that matter to each buyer. Preserve or exceed the existing design and deliver an exceptional complete mobile experience.

## Market scope

User clarified on 10 October 2026: “everything that's EV.” Include fully electric Australian new road vehicles across passenger cars, SUVs, people movers, vans, utes, trucks and buses. Identify specialist and road-registered two-wheel categories explicitly in the unresolved universe; do not claim those closed without audit. Compare within compatible vehicle classes. Keep stock, orderability and future deliveries distinct.

## Acceptance gates

1. Exact-trim strengths and attribute discovery: concise trade-offs; dated supporting evidence; no conflation of a selling point with a market-leading claim; ties preserved; incompatible bases segregated.
2. Complete Australian new-car market audit: every eligible model and trim accounted for using Australian manufacturer and dealer evidence; stock and accepting-orders states recorded independently with check dates. Do not infer stock from a configurator.
3. Design: immutable original captured before editing; before/after screenshots reviewed for each changed screen, including loading/empty/error states. Existing typography, visual language, artwork and motion are the minimum standard.
4. Mobile: full discovery, filters, trim selection, comparison, evidence and share journey passes on real iPhone/Safari and Android/Chrome, as well as narrow viewport, desktop and keyboard tests. Emulation is supplemental, not real-device evidence. Performance tested at expanded catalogue size; reduced motion respected.
5. Trust: a shared fact store feeds all surfaces/exports; claim-specific reports, review queue and correction history; approved corrections recompute rankings; expired/unsupported evidence never retains an unqualified best badge.
