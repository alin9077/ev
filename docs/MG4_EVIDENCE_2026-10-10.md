## Price and ordering qualifications

The table shows [current October promotional driveaway offers](https://mgmotor.com.au/offers), not formal ex-on-road manufacturer list prices. MLP fields remain null. Urban prices already deduct A$2,000 cashback from advertised A$31,990/A$34,990 amounts. The separate A$500 MG4 EV accessory benefit can instead be elected as a purchase-price reduction; it has not been silently subtracted from the headline amounts.

Sale and delivery must occur 1–31 October 2026 at participating dealers, while stocks last. New-car driveaway pricing includes 12-month registration/CTP, maximum dealer delivery and estimated stamp duty; options cost extra. Fleet/government/rental exclusions apply; cashback also excludes hire cars. Neither Urban cashback nor MG4's accessory benefit is freely combinable with other offers. Final location/registration/dealer quotation must be confirmed.

Official [MG4 EV](https://mgmotor.com.au/configurator/mg4-ev) and [Urban](https://mgmotor.com.au/configurator/mg4-ev-urban) configurators offer exact-trim selection, an order form and a refundable A$1,000 order fee. This supports advertised order acceptance, not a completed order or guaranteed delivery. Both default to NSW private registration. Their base-price footnote still says September; the separate offers page explicitly establishes October validity. No orders were attempted.
