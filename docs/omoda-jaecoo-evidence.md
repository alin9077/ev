## Holds and cautions
- MY26 PDF p. 3 says included; current product page disclaimer says not included. Do not publish included or excluded until exact vehicle dealer/manufacturer confirmation.
- PDF says DC Charge (30–80%) 130kW Charger, 30 minutes. This pass preserves charger/test rating separately instead of turning it into a vehicle max DC rating. Likewise never label 30 minutes as 10–80%.
- Specific New stock verified and MY25.5 primary specification exists; dealer labels cross 2026 build year and MY25.5 vehicle descriptions. Separate build, compliance, advertised MY and formal manufacturer spec epoch. MY26 manufacturer release says sole Ultimate, so no MY26 Urban; MY25.5 Ultimate stock mapping could be stale and needs VIN-specific confirmation before unqualified separate-row publication.
- Official AU sheets verify non-braked towing 750 kg and towball 75 kg; no braked rating in cited table. Dealer template 750 kg braked is not adopted.
- Dealer generated navigation, connected-app, dashcam, 280 Nm, and some ventilated-seat claims are excluded; manufacturer table prevails. Current MY26 and MY25.5 show 288 Nm and no front ventilation entitlement.
- Raw-existing 7.7 s and 35 L frunk originate partly in press; not re-certified by current official PDF. Heat pump/V2L absent from reviewed current AU sheet are unknown, not false. Do not carry NZ specs into AU.
- Current page verifies $36,990 October driveaway offer; prior raw $35,990 MRRP not freshly re-certified in this pass.
- Indexed new EX Ferntree CY2010 now redirects to generic stock search, so cached listing not accepted. BX examples were demo/used or NZ. This targeted search did not establish current exact New Australian BX/EX stock; absence of proof is not a national unavailability claim.
