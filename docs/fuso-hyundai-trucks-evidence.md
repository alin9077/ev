## Important integration holds

- all Fuso: 41.3/82.6/123.9 declared on AU page; PDFs round to 41/83/124. Gross/usable and European usable fraction not verified locally
- all Fuso: No comparable, exact-configuration Australian range with cycle/load/body in reviewed current primary sources; leave numeric range unknown
- all Fuso: Theoretical times, no SOC window; never convert to an assumed 10–80 or 0–100 claim
- all Fuso: PDF GCM differs from webpage 0 placeholders. Store PDF declared GCM and downratings; trailer ratings remain unknown
- Hyundai lower-GVM option: Live headline 4,495 kg versus 4,500 kg current PDF/on-page table. Do not silently resolve or count both; exact homologation/plate needs verification
- Hyundai PDF body columns and lower rating: Current PDF repeats 3,250 kg kerb and 4,050 kg payload for 4,500 kg chassis and pantechs. Preserve evidence but reject those payload claims
- Hyundai current multimedia/E-PTO: Current linked sheet lists 8-inch screen, CarPlay/Android Auto and navigation; page/linked brochure still has 7-inch. No ad establishes exact upgraded epoch
- Hyundai: Current specification 13.0 m between kerbs/walls conflicts with 13.6 m release; show qualified figure or hold normalized comparator
- both families: Dealer NEW is evidence only at supported scope. No complete current exact-code/GVM/equipment-epoch match established
- both families: No current list price verified. Do not normalize body-specific dealer prices or 2023 launch pricing into MRLP

Also preserve e615 Wide LWB’s published 89 km/h speed limit; do not apply the other configurations’ 100 km/h value. e515 Wide suspension-seat wording conflicts between its model page and PDF.

## Hyundai charging and payload

The official 114.5 kWh LFP capacity is not labelled gross/usable. Battery voltage is 502 V; the required DC charger compatibility is at least 569.4 V (described as 800 V infrastructure), with 400 V DC explicitly incompatible. Vehicle limits are 6.6 kW AC / 100 kW DC. The 71-minute claim is 8–100% SOC on a 100 kW, 800 V station under test conditions. Its 200 km range reference is local urban testing of a tray loaded to 7.3 tonnes, with air conditioning on.

The current PDF’s 4,050 kg low-GVM and pantech payload cells are not safe completed-vehicle payload figures. Only the 7,300 − 3,250 = 4,050 kg bare-chassis body-plus-load allowance is retained. Current truck warranty is 5 years/200,000 km; HV battery cover is 8 years/400,000 km. The passenger-car seven-year warranty does not apply.
