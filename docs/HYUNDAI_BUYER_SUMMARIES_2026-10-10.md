## Concise buyer copy

### 1. Hyundai ELEXIO FWD 88.1 kWh

- **Strength — Range and luggage room:** The FWD base combines 562 km WLTP range with 506 L VDA luggage space behind the second row.
- **Tradeoff — Front-seat comfort limits:** Front seats lack heating and ventilation; the driver gets six-way power adjustment without memory.
- Sources: [elexio_configurator](https://www.hyundai.com/content/api/au/hyundai/v3/carpricecalculator?postcode=2000&displaypowertrain=true&modelname=elexio); [elexio_specs](https://www.hyundai.com/content/dam/hyundai/au/en/models/elexio/docs/Hyundai_ELEXIO_Specification_Sheet.pdf)

### 2. Hyundai ELEXIO Elite FWD 88.1 kWh

- **Strength — Adjustable seat comfort:** Heated and ventilated front seats, 14-way power driver adjustment and driver-seat memory make the Elite a comfort-focused choice.
- **Tradeoff — Wheel-specific range reduction:** The Elite’s 20-inch configuration is rated at 546 km WLTP, versus 562 km for the 18-inch base; both remain 160 kW FWD.
- Sources: [elexio_specs](https://www.hyundai.com/content/dam/hyundai/au/en/models/elexio/docs/Hyundai_ELEXIO_Specification_Sheet.pdf); [elexio_configurator](https://www.hyundai.com/content/api/au/hyundai/v3/carpricecalculator?postcode=2000&displaypowertrain=true&modelname=elexio)

### 3. Hyundai IONIQ 9 Calligraphy AWD 7-seat 110.3 kWh

- **Strength — Seven-seat family utility:** Seven seats and a published 2,500 kg braked towing limit give the Calligraphy a clear large-family and trailer-carrying role.
- **Tradeoff — Large footprint, occupied-row boot:** The body is 5,060 mm long, while luggage space behind the third row is 338 L VDA, so check parking and fully occupied luggage needs.
- Sources: [ioniq9_specs_my27](https://www.hyundai.com/content/dam/hyundai/au/en/models/ioniq-9-2025/docs/Hyundai-IONIQ-9-Specification-sheet-my27.pdf)

### 4. Hyundai IONIQ 9 Calligraphy AWD 6-seat 110.3 kWh

- **Strength — Second-row captain-seat comfort:** Power-adjustable second-row captain seats bring relaxation functions, heating and ventilation for passengers who value individual-seat comfort.
- **Tradeoff — One fewer passenger place:** This version carries six rather than seven people, yet keeps the same 5,060 mm length and 338 L VDA behind the third row.
- Sources: [ioniq9_specs_my27](https://www.hyundai.com/content/dam/hyundai/au/en/models/ioniq-9-2025/docs/Hyundai-IONIQ-9-Specification-sheet-my27.pdf)

### 5. Hyundai INSTER Standard Range 42 kWh FWD

- **Strength — Small footprint, flexible rear seats:** A 3,825 mm body and 50:50 sliding, flat-folding rear seats suit buyers who want a compact footprint with adjustable cabin space.
- **Tradeoff — Range and comfort limits:** WLTP range is 327 km; front seats lack heating and ventilation, and a surround-view camera is not fitted.
- Sources: [inster_specs_aug2026](https://www.hyundai.com/content/dam/hyundai/au/en/models/inster/MY26-INSTER-Specification-Sheet-Aug-2026.pdf)

### 6. Hyundai INSTER Cross 49 kWh FWD with sunroof
