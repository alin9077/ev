# Version B scenes, as they stood on 8 October 2026

A copy of the seven B animations before they were rewritten to the revised
story brief (retail pricing and charging cost, Supercharging, storage,
acoustic glass, software updates).

- `b/` is `src/lib/scenes/b/`.
- `chapter-scenes.ts` and `scene-readout.ts` are from `src/lib/`.
- `scenes.test.ts` is from `tests/`.
- `SCENE_DIRECTION_B.md` is from `docs/`.
- `index-b.html` is the built page: open it to see these animations as they were.

To restore: copy `b/` back over `src/lib/scenes/b/`, put the three source
files back where they came from, then run `pnpm build`.
