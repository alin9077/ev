## Important corrections and exclusions
- Polestar 2 nomenclature: MY 27 AU configurator explicitly FE/FD/FF Long range names; homepage calls the rear-drive example Rear motor. Alias, not fourth grade.
- Volvo nominal battery vs gross/usable: Nominal retained, gross and usable null; never reverse-engineer from energy/voltage/Ah or use foreign sheets.
- Volvo charging: DC station rating and 10–80% duration retained; vehicle peak null. ES 90 dealer 350 kW condition is separate from manufacturer 300 kW condition.
- Volvo price display: Unlocalized MSRP/DA fields and relative price differences do not establish national all-tax driveaway; exact selected base displays kept with tax uncertainty.
- EX 40 Single dealer range: South Yarra prose says 576 km, while current official and dealer spec table say 520. Manufacturer 520 retained; dealer prose rejected. Dealer Wh/km labels also appear scaled incorrectly and were not imported.
- Polestar 3 energy consumption: Main MY 27 AU spec vs configurator numbers differ; normalized consumption null with both claims recorded.
- Volvo EX 30 Cross Country: Height and cargo are not copied from regular EX 30; exact dealer height 1567/tare 1910 kept separate from manufacturer running-order 1960.
- MY 26 EX 30 stock identity: Dealer explicitly calls stock 13702716 MY 26 in two places despite calendar 2026/VINV; no automatic VIN or year inference.
- EX 90 stock cache: Search returns MY 27 new unit; open returns last-year MY 25 list. Neither promoted without exact fresh detail.
- Polestar 5 order status: AU MY 26 Launch edition listing/configuration is real but disableBtoOption=true. Current stock and order acceptance unresolved; audit-only.
- P 4 coupe vs SUV: Separate MY 27 body variants with different height/range/weight; no mixed cargo or rear-window claims. Performance is optional same-power Dual motor configuration; its explicit target range is 500 km coupé/520 km SUV.
- Polestar 4 Performance range recovered: AU MY 27 configurator explicitly keys range-performance-wltp-min-in-kilometer 500 km coupe/520 kmSUV and XPFWHE range map matches. Consumption 21.7/21.6 applies to Performance; baseDual family bands include this endpoint and are not exact base wheel claims.

- Polestar roadside assistance: Current product-page headings/specification say 5 years but same current page bodies say 3; normalized term null with observations retained.

## Useful ownership/equipment distinctions
- EX 30 Ultra adds panoramic glass and parking-assistance/360-camera highlights over Plus; Cross Country has raised ride height and P 8 AWD
- EX 40 Ultra manufacturer highlights include panoramic glass, 360 camera, Harman Kardon, Pilot Assist, Pixel LED headlights and heated rear seats
- EX 90/ES 90 Ultra adds expensive comfort hardware including air suspension, upgraded audio and electrochromic glass; EX 90 is seven seats, ES 90 five
- MY 27 Polestar 3 is 800 V; full DCpeak 310 kWRear or 350 kWDual/Performance, versus 120 kW on 400 V supply. Its newer specification must not be blended with older 400 V 111 kWh vehicles
- MY 27 Polestar 4 has 11 kWAC as standard;22 kW is a separate option requiring a suitable cable and three-phase supply. SUV and coupé have different bodies; SUV has rear window/wiper and roof rails
- Polestar 4 Performance pack changes wheels/brakes/dampers, not 400 kW output. The AU configurator identifies 22-inch Performance WLTP targets of 500 km coupé/520 km SUV; exact pack mass remains null
- Volvo battery warranty:8 years/160,000 km and 70%capacity floor; vehicle 5 years/unlimitedkm fromfirstregistration. Polestar current spec states 5-year vehicle and 8-year/160,000 km/70%battery; Polestar 2 MY 27 battery terms are corroborated by the current every-Polestar 2 product warranty despite stale MY 25/MY 26 specification heading. Polestar roadside term is null because current page body says 3 years but headings/spec say 5
