## Conflicts, recall and rejected leads
- E07 brochure: 13 h 0–100% AC at 7 kW. Website prose: about 16 h. Keep conflict visible. Website summary 3.9s acceleration versus exact brochure 3.96 s AWD.
- E07 current sheet marks air suspension standard for both; website narrative highlights AWD. Confirm older delivered build rather than back-applying the September 2026 sheet.
- [REC-006560](https://www.vehiclerecalls.gov.au/recalls/rec-006560), published 10 March 2026, covers 218 listed 2025 SC6501 vehicles because the centre-rear child-restraint anchor may be missing. Check delivered VIN/rectification before relying on current three-anchor fitment. No stock VIN is asserted affected or cleared. Direct VIN-list download timed out.
- RWD Brisbane 23788 redirects to index; Waverley D1094 and D1071 are SOLD in direct bodies. Inchcape DE500064A redirects to index; cached detail was storage-compound stock with 5–10-day arrival estimate. Cached NEW snippets cannot override live rejection.
