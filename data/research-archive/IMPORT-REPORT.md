# Import report: Dot research into the app's data slots

Generated by `scripts/import-dot-research.ts` from `export` (Dot's "Ford32" cut, evidence 2026-10-05 to 2026-10-16). Structural normalisation only: no value was re-verified.

## What was loaded

- 433 variants: the original 34 plus 399 new, in 183 new model/generation records. The app's ids, slugs and routes are unchanged for the original 34; new ids follow `<model_id>__<trim_slug>__<n>` (Dot's date-suffixed and two-part ids are rewritten; the old ids are in `id-map.json`). No (family, slug) route repeats.
- 9526 facts in the app's 22 fact slots (every variant has all 22; unknown is null, never zero).
- 5286 stored equipment rows for the new variants; 22245 rows that are still "needs confirmation" are not stored and expand from a per-variant default. Every variant has all 69 criteria.
- 1096 new sources in the app (deduplicated by URL: 946 duplicate ids merged), 1392 more in `sources-archive.json` (cited only by archived content). The original 424 sources are unchanged.
- 28887 facts under 7034 further fact keys went to `extra-facts.json`; 86 of them also filled an empty slot, 812 filled an existing variant-record field.
